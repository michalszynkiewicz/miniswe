### Shape

- **Shell-first.** 55 of 129 calls are `shell`: `grep -n`, `sed -n`, `head`,
  `find`, three `python3 << 'EOF'` heredocs that rewrite files, and two
  `sed -i` edits. That means ~5 of its edits went around miniswe's edit
  machinery (diff-echo, AST guard, revert history) entirely. It worked here;
  on a harder task the guards would be blind to those edits. Worth a
  system-prompt nudge ("edit with the edit tools, not sed/python") if it
  recurs — or accept it as the model's style.
- **Schema slips on `shell`**: 4 of the 9 failures were `{"action":"find …"}`
  / `{"action":"grep …"}` — the command put into `action` instead of
  `action:"run", command:…`. Recovered immediately each time.
- **Plan**: it wrote a markdown plan with headers that parsed into 23
  "steps" (all compile-gated), then at the end checked off steps 1..23 one
  per round (24 `plan` calls, ~1 min). Harmless, but the step parser turned
  `# Plan:` / bullet headers into steps.
- Edit discipline otherwise fine: 14 `replace_range` with 5 reverts, 1
  successful `refactor(add_param)` on the CLI struct after one validator
  rejection.
